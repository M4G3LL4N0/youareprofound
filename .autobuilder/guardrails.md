# Autobuilder Guardrails

## Product Truth

YouAreProfound is a premium meaning operating system, not a generic quote site.

## Public Positioning

Public copy should describe structured wisdom, reflection, meaning, peace, clarity, and personal understanding.

## Do Not Expose

Do not expose internal Autobuilder details, private founder details, unlicensed content claims, or fake official affiliation with Alan Watts/TED.

## Do Not Delete

Do not delete source, used assets, configs, pnpm lockfile, docs, or Autobuilder foundation files.

## Do Not Drift Toward

Do not drift toward generic SaaS, Noaerth clone, meditation clone, random quote scraper, therapy replacement, or fake AI claims.

## Safe Improvements

Visual polish, responsive design, content expansion, static demo flows, path structure, SEO, accessibility, build fixes.

## Risky Improvements

Auth, database, paid subscriptions, AI API integration, licensed content ingestion, automatic deploys, automatic pushes.

## Build Rules

Use pnpm only. Do not use npm. Do not deploy automatically. Do not push automatically. Keep Vercel deploy manual.
